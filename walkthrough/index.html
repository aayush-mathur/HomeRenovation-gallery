Explore at eye level

Look: drag anywhere in the view. On desktop, Mouse look captures the pointer; Esc releases it.

Settings: ceilings, switchboards, pace, mouse look, lens and offline copies are grouped here. Close, press Esc or tap the house to return to exploring. Room and Full screen stay in the top bar, including in full screen.

Lens: lower millimetres give a wider view without moving you. The 16–70 mm slider uses 35 mm-equivalent focal lengths with a fixed 36 mm horizontal film gate; viewport height follows its aspect ratio. Auto preserves the original 62° vertical view, capped at 90° horizontally. Reset lens restores Auto, not your position. Wide views stretch edges, especially in portrait; this is not a simulation of human eyesight. Release Mouse look with Esc before using the slider.

Ceiling: Sculpted Luxe is a historical visualization, not the approved ceiling or lighting layout. Your designer will finalize the ceiling. Functional lighting groups are reviewed separately. Fans are static provisions, not rotating. Web lighting and emissive faces are approximate.

Starting view: each fresh visit opens the historical Luxe visualization with provisional switchboards, nearby board labels and solid appliance cords. The ceiling is not approved. Settings → Appliance cords lists physical plugs/flex, missing supplies and joinery clashes. View socket close-up moves you to a safe nearby position at 1.6 m eye height and uses a 45 mm inspection lens; Reset lens restores a wider view. Transparent furniture reveals concealed cords without making walls transparent. Socket/adapter shapes, device inlets and cabinet entries are proposals, not approved installation details. Lighting & connections separately shows dashed relationships, not physical wires or safe routes. If an overlay cannot load, an explicit warning and retry remain available. Saving an offline copy still requires the Download button.

Walk: click or tab into the view or movement pad, then use W A S D or arrow keys. Q E turn; R F look up/down. Touch: hold and drag the thumbstick to walk; drag farther to walk faster. Release to stop. Use a second finger on the house to look while moving.

Pace: switch Walk (1.35 m/s) to Faster (2 m/s) with the pace button. The thumbstick still gives fine control near its centre. On desktop, hold Shift while walking for temporary Faster pace; release it to return to your selected pace. Both paces pass through furniture for inspection, but not walls or floor boundaries. Each page visit starts in Walk.

Finger + pen: hold the movement pad with a finger and drag the house with the pen tip, in either order. Pen hover does not turn the view. Some tablets reject finger input when a pen is nearby; a website cannot override that device behavior. If movement stops, lift the pen and touch the pad again, or use two fingers. Cancelled finger input always stops walking safely.

Optional troubleshooting: records only pointer type/ID and start/stop/focus reasons in this page's memory. No coordinates, personal data, storage or upload. Reopen Help after reproducing the problem to read the last 20 events; turn recording off to clear them.
