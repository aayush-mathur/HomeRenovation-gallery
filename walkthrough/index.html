Explore at eye level

Look: drag anywhere in the view. On desktop, Mouse look captures the pointer; Esc releases it.

Full screen: controls tuck away so the house fills the screen. Tap Show controls for rooms, ceilings, lens and Exit full screen. Hide controls or tap the house to tuck them away again. Esc also exits full screen in supported browsers.

Lens: lower millimetres give a wider view without moving you. The 16–70 mm slider uses 35 mm-equivalent focal lengths with a fixed 36 mm horizontal film gate; viewport height follows its aspect ratio. Auto preserves the original 62° vertical view, capped at 90° horizontally. Reset lens restores Auto, not your position. Wide views stretch edges, especially in portrait; this is not a simulation of human eyesight. Release Mouse look with Esc before using the slider.

Ceiling concepts: switch the whole floor, including bedrooms, without moving your view. Existing restores the original ceiling fixtures. Fans are static provisions, not rotating. Web lighting and emissive faces are approximate; use Cycles reference previews for lighting comparisons.

Walk: click or tab into the view, then use W A S D or arrow keys. Q E turn; R F look up/down. Touch: drag to look and hold a movement arrow.

Walk between rooms: interior doors are held open on their real hinges, with leaves and handles intact. Walk through doorways normally; the room menu is just an optional shortcut.

Storage opening is disabled: cupboards and drawers stay in their closed, fixed positions while you explore. Click/tap only focuses the view; dragging looks around. Furniture and room layouts are retained.

Narrow aisles: use sideways steps around beds and furniture. The preview uses a 40 cm round body; narrow furniture aisles are not an accessible-design approval. Reset position returns to your selected starting room.

1.6 m eye height. Simplified real-time materials and lighting, not Cycles. Mirrors are material approximations, not real reflections. Walking stays on this floor: stair flights are visual only, not climbable. The rear OTS has a provisional open metal grate at floor level with the shaft void below; grating, supports and glazing are visualization only, not certified load-bearing construction.
