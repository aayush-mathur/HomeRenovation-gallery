Explore at eye level

Look: drag anywhere in the view. On desktop, Mouse look captures the pointer; Esc releases it.

Full screen: use the toolbar button to expand the walkthrough. Exit full screen or Esc returns to the normal view.

Walk: click or tab into the view, then use W A S D or arrow keys. Q E turn; R F look up/down. Touch: drag to look and hold a movement arrow.

Walk between rooms: interior doors are held open on their real hinges, with leaves and handles intact. Walk through doorways normally; the room menu is just an optional shortcut.

Narrow aisles: use sideways steps around beds and furniture. The preview uses a 40 cm round body; the middle bedroom's roughly 45 cm bed-end gap is tight, not an accessible-design approval. Reset position returns to your selected starting room.

1.6 m eye height. Simplified real-time materials and lighting, not Cycles. Mirrors are material approximations, not real reflections. Provisional geometry remains unverified. Exterior exits, unmodeled stairs and the floorless rear OTS are blocked.
